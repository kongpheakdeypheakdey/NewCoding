Our Departments

Explore 12 specialized departments at Luang Mè Hospital, each focused on expert care and patient comfort.

Outpatient Department (OPD)

First-stop care for consultations, follow-ups and general outpatient services.

Emergency Department

24/7 urgent care, rapid response and critical stabilization for emergency cases.

Medical Imaging & Radiology

Advanced imaging services for accurate diagnosis using X-ray, CT, MRI and ultrasound.

Oncology & Radiotherapy

Comprehensive cancer diagnosis and treatment with supportive radiation therapy care.

Laboratory Department (Lab)

Reliable medical testing and rapid lab results to support clinical decision-making.

Anesthesia & Recovery

Safe anesthesia services with attentive post-operative recovery monitoring.

Gynecology Department

Women’s health care including maternity support, screenings and reproductive wellness.

Surgical Department

Expert surgical teams providing general and specialized procedures with modern techniques.

Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

Critical care support with continuous monitoring for seriously ill and recovering patients.

Pharmacy

Prescription services, medication counseling and fast pharmaceutical support for patients.

Endoscopy Department

Specialized endoscopic exams for digestive health and minimally invasive diagnostics.

Rehabilitation Services

Rehab programs for recovery, mobility improvement and long-term physical wellness.
